Excited to share a data-driven prediction for the **2026 Tamil Nadu Assembly Election**! 🗳️📊

Using Python and Machine Learning, I built a predictive model to analyze the shifting political landscape with the entry of **Tamizhaga Vetri Kazhagam (TVK)**.

**🔍 The Approach:**
*   **Historical Analysis (1991-2021)**: Analyzed 30 years of vote share oscillations between Dravidian majors (DMK & AIADMK) using **Polynomial Regression**.
*   **New Entrant Modeling**: Integrated recent perception surveys and opinion polls to estimate TVK's impact (projected ~25% vote share).
*   **Market Share Normalization**: Adjusted the established parties' baselines to account for the new 3-way contest equilibrium.

**📈 The Prediction (2026):**
The model forecasts a high-stakes 3-cornered battle:
*   **DMK Alliance**: ~33.4%
*   **AIADMK Alliance**: ~31.6%
*   **TVK**: ~25.0%

This suggests a potential **hung assembly** or a very tight race, signaling a major disruption in Tamil Nadu's traditional bi-polar politics.
