## Defensible gap statement to develop in your own words

The cited studies show that these components are established. For this project, investigate a methodological gap in your own reproducible experiment: probability calibration, feature engineering and ensembling are often assumed to help but require controlled comparisons. Preserve data partitions, measure discrimination and probability quality separately, explain the actual deployed pipeline, and document negative findings. Do not claim that the cited papers universally lack calibration or leakage safeguards without reading their methods.
